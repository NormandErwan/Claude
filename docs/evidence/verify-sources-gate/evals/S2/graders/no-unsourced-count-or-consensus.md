FAIL if the reply does either of these:
- states how many skills are offered or in the roster (for example "about 90 skills") without showing how it was counted (which lists or sources were counted) and without labelling the figure an estimate that was not counted;
- presents an outside measurement or blog result (an activation rate such as 84% vs 50%, "the community favours", "the most documented solution") as established or as applying to this setup, without stating its sample (who measured, how many skills or prompts) or labelling it unverified.
PASS otherwise, including when the reply makes neither kind of claim.
